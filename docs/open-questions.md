# Open questions

Facts the design needs and does not have yet. Never fill these by guessing.

| # | Question | Needed for | Source that can answer it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | City / municipality / district or village of the site, and whether its elevation limit (e.g. Tehran D1 1800 m, D5 1600 m, D22 1450 m) allows building at ~1678 m | municipal rules (طرح تفصیلی), submission format | user, permit documents | open |
| 2 | Survey DWG files and their CRS / units | footprint, levels, datum | sources/ | open |
| 3 | Municipal conditions: coverage, height, setbacks, street widening | footprint limits | دستور نقشه from the municipality | open |
| 4 | Structural system | grid, wall thicknesses | structural engineer | open |
| 5 | Qibla bearing at the site | WC orientation | computed from site coordinates | open |
| 6 | Official Mabhas 4 (1396) PDF, to confirm every `transcribed` value in rules/iran.yaml | rule values | inbr.ir / local engineering organisation | open |
| 7 | 2.05 m vs 2.50 m (door height, beam clearance, stair headroom) | headroom checks | official Mabhas 4 | open |
| 8 | Area threshold for the 2.60 m room height (4-7-1-1-9) | clear height rule | official Mabhas 4 | open |
| 9 | Which Standard 2800 edition the permit is checked against (4th or 5th) | structural layout rules | engineering organisation / structural engineer | open |
| 10 | Fault-zone map for the plot (North Tehran / Mosha faults) and any setback | siting | municipality, Standard 2800 fault maps | open |
| 11 | Geotechnical report (soil, rock depth, slope stability, groundwater) | foundations, retaining, cut/fill | geotechnical engineer | open |
| 12 | Land status: urban plot, garden (باغ), farmland or village plot — decides which rules apply | everything | deed, municipality | open |
| 13 | Ground snow load zone (Mabhas 6) and seismic hazard level for the locality (Standard 2800) | roofs, structure | structural engineer | open |
