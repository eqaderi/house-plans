# ADR 0021: Two-dimensional space placement with CP-SAT NoOverlap2D

Status: accepted (Stage S7, closed 2026-10-18). Extends
[ADR 0013](0013-solver-backed-plan-generation.md): bar layouts remain; this adds
free 2D placement.

## Context

Before Stage S7, plans were generated by a 1D interval decomposition
(`generation/solver.py`, `massing.py`). The footprint was a set of fixed
orthogonal bars (`linear`, `compact`, `split_level`, `two_wing`, `courtyard`),
each bar was split into longitudinal rows (`front`, `back`, `corridor`), and
clustered spaces were packed along a row with CP-SAT `AddNoOverlap`. That is
fast, but every bar needed a full-length corridor, every space in a row took the
row's depth, and open-plan, L-shaped and courtyard layouts could not place
spaces freely. Flexible generation needs spaces placed in x and y with
shared-wall topology constraints.

## Considered options

Three CP-SAT formulations were benchmarked on synthetic space programs
(`tools/benchmark_nooverlap2d.py`, macOS arm64, OR-Tools 9.15.6755, one worker,
`seed=42`, deterministic budget 5.0). Statuses, objectives and model sizes below
were reproduced by a re-run on 2026-11-17; wall times vary between runs.

1. **`NoOverlap2D`**: the native global constraint `AddNoOverlap2D(x_intervals, y_intervals)`.
2. **Pairwise disjunctions**: for every pair, `AddBoolOr([left, right, below, above])`.
3. **1D row intervals**: the existing row partition with `AddNoOverlap` per row.

- Both 2D formulations find more compact layouts than rows (objective 201 vs
  206 for 9 spaces, 273 and 269 vs 291 for 14), because rows force spaces into
  strips and fill what is left. Rows prove optimality fastest.
- `NoOverlap2D` grows linearly (52, 91 and 136 variables for 5, 9 and 14
  spaces). Pairwise disjunctions grow quadratically (92 to 500 variables and
  132 to 664 constraints), adding Booleans that the native constraint's
  box-packing propagators avoid.
- Repeated runs with the same seed gave identical coordinates and objectives.

## Decision

Place spaces with CP-SAT `AddNoOverlap2D`, choose each space's dimensions from
a table of allowed sizes, and express shared walls as linear constraints:

1. **Sizes by `AddAllowedAssignments`.** Each space gets integer candidate
   pairs $(w_k, h_k)$ whose area lies between 85 % and 125 % of its target
   (and within its program minimum and maximum), with its minimum width and an
   aspect ratio of at most 2.5 (`generation/solver2d.py`,
   `constants/placement2d.py`).
2. **No overlap by `AddNoOverlap2D`** on $X_i = [x_i, x_i + w_i]$ and
   $Y_i = [y_i, y_i + h_i]$ for every space on a level.
3. **Shared walls as linear constraints.** Contact of at least
   $L_{\min} = 0.9$ m (9 dm) between spaces $A$ and $B$ is one of four
   orientations:
   - East: $x_a + w_a = x_b \land y_a + h_a \ge y_b + L_{\min} \land y_b + h_b \ge y_a + L_{\min}$
   - West: $x_b + w_b = x_a \land y_a + h_a \ge y_b + L_{\min} \land y_b + h_b \ge y_a + L_{\min}$
   - North: $y_a + h_a = y_b \land x_a + w_a \ge x_b + L_{\min} \land x_b + w_b \ge x_a + L_{\min}$
   - South: $y_b + h_b = y_a \land x_a + w_a \ge x_b + L_{\min} \land x_b + w_b \ge x_a + L_{\min}$

   A hard `adjacent` space relationship requires
   $T_{ab}^{\text{east}} + T_{ab}^{\text{west}} + T_{ab}^{\text{north}} + T_{ab}^{\text{south}} \ge 1$;
   a soft one penalises $(1 - T_{ab})$ in the objective.
4. **No mandatory corridor.** Spaces may connect directly; circulation is added
   only where direct connections cannot form a route tree to the entry.

## Consequences

- Bar layouts (ADR 0013) and free 2D placement exist side by side as
  generation strategies.
- On larger programs the solver stops at the deterministic budget with a
  feasible layout rather than a proven optimum (gaps of 38–52 % in the
  benchmark).
