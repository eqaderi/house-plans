# 25 · Household operations, logistics and storage: review of C10

## Summary

1. Daily logistics are mostly sound (car to lift 4 m indoors, step-free road door, laundry beside the bedrooms).
2. Laundry, drying and store share one buried 13.9 m² room (rule 6.7 broken), and there is no outdoor drying.
3. Shoes-off is not planned at the garage, guest or garden doors.
4. 15 guests work. 30–35 need the furniture cleared and stored somewhere. 60 do not fit indoors. There is one guest WC.
5. No bin store, outdoor cooking or LPG place, garden store or main-floor cleaning cupboard. The store is about 4.5 m³ short.
