# 22 · Natural hazards and resilience: review of C10

Concept-stage review of hazards and outages (structural seismic design excluded). Nothing here is engineered or geotechnically verified.

## Summary

1. The hill is the largest hazard. C10 has three high retaining conditions (road and forecourt above, the cut against the north neighbour, the downhill fills), and none has a construction method or drainage.
2. Snow has no plan. Shovelled snow has nowhere to go, the terraces sit 20 mm below their doors, cornices form over the front door, and two outdoor stairs will ice.
3. Only the stair works in a power cut. There is no lift rescue, no backup heat, and too little water, stored in the wrong place.
4. After an earthquake there are no reachable shut-offs, no assembly point and no emergency store.
5. Two cheap wins: a rainwater cistern built in place of the garden-terrace fill, and gravity greywater to the garden.

## Finding details

**RES-01 · Retaining walls at the road.** *Measured:* the road edge is at 1677.51–1677.57. The forecourt carries up to 3.0 m of fill (39 m³) and drops 3.8 m at v 11.6 to court-w (1673.60). Digging the court west to the road (rule 4.10) creates a wall 3.9–4.0 m high holding a public road. *Correction:* use reinforced-concrete counterfort walls founded below court level, designed for traffic surcharge and seismic earth pressure. Add drainage behind them and build in alternating bays of ≤ 3 m under shoring. Put a 1.1 m vehicle and pedestrian barrier on top. Carry the forecourt on a suspended slab or on engineered, benched fill. 

**RES-02 · North cut.** *Measured:* the formation is at 1669.65, and the ground at (6.8, 0.2) is 1673.75. That is a 4.1 m cut 1.1 m from uphill land. *Correction:* before digging, build an embedded wall (contiguous piles, or soil nails with shotcrete in 1.5 m lifts) along v 1.1 from u 5.0 to u 13.0. Dig only in June–September.

**RES-03 · Downhill fills.** *Measured:* 2.64 m of fill under t-low and 1.35 m under the lawn, on ground falling 35–38 %. The tree bank to the east boundary is only 2.5–3.6 m wide. *Correction:* bench every fill into the slope in 0.5 m steps, with geogrid and a heel drain. Discharge no roof water into the fill. For t-low, see RES-13.

**RES-04 · Run-off and mud.** *Measured:* the forecourt (1677.40) sits 0.11–0.17 m below the road edge, so road water runs to the shutters and the front door (1677.50). The shared footpath lies above court-w west of u −0.7 (1674.06 at u −2), and up to 3.0 m above it once rule 4.10 is applied. The north neighbour drains onto the north wall at v 1.1. *Correction:*
- A 300 mm trench drain at the parcel line, with the forecourt falling 1.5 % from the garage (1677.48) to it.
- A 150 mm upstand and channel along the court's south edge.
- A swale in the north setback leading east to a level spreader at the bank.

**RES-05 · Snow storage.** *Measured:* the forecourt, gstair and court cover 77.2 m². A 0.4 m snowfall (*assumption*) gives about 31 m³ of loose snow. The apron in front of the north shutter is 1.8 m deep, so the road plough's berm lands on the door. *Correction:*
- A drained snow pit 2.0 × 3.0 × 1.5 m (9 m³) at the court's NW corner, with a hinged 1.2 m gate in the forecourt railing above it.
- Main-floor terrace snow goes over the balc-m balustrade (north end), never south onto path-s.

**RES-06 · Thresholds.** *Measured:* each terrace sits 20 mm below its floor (deck-r 50 mm). The ENE sliders face the most frequent winter wind (E, 26 % of December–February hours; REPORT), so drifted snow melts into the rooms. *Correction:* keep the finishes step-free (rule 5.4), but drop the structural slab 180 mm. Use pavers on pedestals and a 150 mm grated channel at every slider.

**RES-07 · Cornices and icicles.** *Inferred:* the flat road roof's west edge is leeward of the east wind, so a cornice will hang over the shutters and the front door (u 0, v 10.15–11.35). Its east edge hangs over the lobby glass door (u 11.4). The main entry door (u 2, v 11.55–12.75) has no cover. *Correction:* internal drains in heated space, 0.6 m parapets with snow guards, and 1.5 m canopies over all three doors.

**RES-08 · Icing stairs.** *Inferred:*
- **gstair** is shaded by the road block in the morning and by the bank (1675.9 at u −5) in the afternoon, so it gets about 2 h of December sun.
- **gs-lawn** lies wholly under balc-e and is open to the east wind.
- **path-s** is 1.0 m wide, falls 33 % (3.8 m over 11.4 m) and runs under the terrace drip edge.

*Correction:*
- **gstair:** a 1.6 m canopy, and 24 risers ≤ 0.16 m with goings ≥ 0.30 m and a landing at riser 12. Handrails on both sides and gritted treads.
- **gs-lawn:** a glazed east screen.
- **path-s:** stepped, with landings every 10–12 risers, and the terrace upstand sending its water to internal drains.

**RES-09 · Water.** *Inferred:* the 6.1 m² plant room holds about 2 m³ of tanks. For 6–8 people at 150 L/p/d (*assumption*), that lasts 1.7–2.2 days. Gravity head from 1677.5 gives about 0.54 bar at a lower-floor shower but only 0.19 bar on the main floor. *Correction:*
- Store at least 4 m³: 1 m³ at road level as a gravity reserve for the lower floor, and the rest in a buried tank under court-w with a 0.75 kW booster pump on backup power.
- Enlarge the plant room to at least 9 m², and strap the tanks.

**RES-10 · Power cuts.** *Correction:*
- **Lift:** an automatic rescue device that brings the car to the nearest floor.
- **Boiler:** boiler and circulator (0.25 kW) on battery backup.
- **Drainage:** gravity only, with no sump pumps. The perimeter drain at 1669.3 can outfall at u 20.6.
- **PV:** about 8 kWp on the road roof (40 m² at 25–30°, facing SSE). *Inferred:* about 20 kWh/day in January; simulation needed.
- **Battery:** 10–15 kWh in a fire-separated cupboard of 1.5 m², away from the water.
- **Generator:** a 32 A inlet on the west façade, with the generator run outdoors only.

**RES-11 · Gas cut.** *Correction:* run one 250 × 250 mm flue chase from the lower lounge (about 12.8, 7.5) above the road roof for a 6–8 kW wood stove.

**RES-12 · After an earthquake.** *Measured:* there is a 1.1 m pier between the south shutter and the front door (v 9.03–10.15). *Correction:*
- **Shut-off niche:** a 0.6 × 0.9 m niche in that pier for the gas meter, a seismic gas shut-off (*assumption*: verify), the water stop-cock and the main isolator, all reachable from outside. Add water valves on each floor.
- **Assembly points:** the road, and the NE lawn at u 24.9–28.2, v 0.6–8.0 (12.4 m², *measured*). That lawn area is at least 5.5 m (half the 11 m parapet height) from balc-e.
- **Emergency store:** one garage locker as a 72-hour kit with 72 L of water.

**RES-13 · Rainwater and greywater.** *Measured:* the road roof and deck-r give 166 m² of catchment, which at a run-off coefficient of 0.8 yields 13.3 m³ per 100 mm of rain, or about 43 m³ a year at the unverified NASA figure of 325 mm. *Correction:*
- **Cistern:** a concrete cistern R(18.2, 3.0, 20.4, 11.4), 1.6 m deep (29.6 m³), replacing the 38.9 m³ of terrace fill. Its outlet at 1668.3 drip-feeds the garden by gravity and supplies a fire hose.
- **Greywater:** greywater from sh-l, lav, laundry and gens (0.3–0.4 m³/day) runs by gravity to subsurface drip in June–September only, and to the sewer from October to May.

**RES-14 · Wildfire.** *Measured:* July relative humidity is 20 % at a 28 °C mean. *Correction:*
- A 1.5 m gravel strip around the house, then 10 m of irrigated, low-fuel planting.
- Deciduous broadleaf trees only (walnut, almond, mulberry), with crowns at least 3 m from the façade.
- No timber decks.
- 3 mm ember mesh on vents.

**RES-15 · Lightning.** *ai_judgment:* the road roof is the high point of the slope. *Correction:* cast a foundation earth electrode into the footings now. Add a roof air-termination ring, down-conductors at 4 corners, and Type 1+2 surge protection for the main board, PV and lift.

**RES-16 · Wind.** *Inferred:* balc-e is about 5 m above the ground and deck-r about 11 m above the lawn. *Correction:* 1.1 m glass balustrades, a 1.8 m screen at the NNW end of balc-m, and anchored furniture and PV.

**RES-17 · Roof.** *Correction:* add a 0.9 × 0.9 m hatch above the stair's REST landing. The ground snow load for Paveh comes from INBR Part 6 (*assumption*: 1.5–3.0 kPa), plus drift on deck-r and green-r against the 3.4 m step.

**RES-18 · Rockfall.** Unknown. *Needed:* photos of the slope west of the road. If it has rock faces higher than 3 m, the garage's west wall becomes solid reinforced concrete.

## What C10 gets right

- One stair serves all three levels without electricity (rule 5.2).
- Service rooms fill the buried NW, so no bedroom stands against retained earth.
- The lower floor meets the ground on the east, so it drains by gravity with no pumps.
- The natural tree bank is kept, and the two downhill walls are at least 5 m apart.

## Out-of-scope notes

- Stairs: gs-court drops 2.3 m in 2.0 m.
- Vehicle access: the north shutter's apron is 1.8 m deep.
- Structure: heavy tanks and the green roof sit at the top of the building.
- Envelope: terraces over the bedrooms need waterproofing.
