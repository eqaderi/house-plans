# 05 · Site access, arrival and external stairs (C10)

Concept-stage review on the inferred TIN; nothing is engineered or code-checked. Stair criteria are `assumption`: 2R+G 600–650 mm, riser ≤ 175 mm, ≤ 12–16 risers per flight, guards 1.1 m where the drop exceeds 0.6 m.

## Summary

1. The owner is right twice. The court→garden link drops 2.30 m in 2.0 m (115 %), a ladder, and then a 1.02 m strip of 32 % slope with no steps.
2. The road stair is unsafe: 22 risers in one flight, no bottom landing, open drops of 3.8 m. It also wastes a 32.2 m² wedge of 47–51 % slope.
3. The owner's proposal works: an L-stair of 24 × 158 mm against the road, and a court of 51.6 m² instead of 34.0 m² (+52 %). Maximum cut 3.15 m; about 57 m³ extra.
4. A gentle garden stair (40 × 150 mm, 17.9 m of run) fits only if it starts inside the court at u −0.4. From there it follows natural ground within ±0.45 m to the lawn.
5. Also: road water drains into the court, the 14 lawn steps are steep, the road door has a 100 mm step, and there is no bin store.

## What C10 gets right

- Both front doors are on the west façade (rule 2.6), and the road door leads straight to the lift.
- Groceries go from garage to lift in 4 m, all indoors (rule 5.1), and the garage apron falls to the road.
- The garden terrace is step-free from the lift through the lounge (20 mm threshold).
- The arrival court at the main-floor level gives guests an entrance without going through the road hall.
- The lawn sits on the contour at natural height, so the garden stair can end at grade.
