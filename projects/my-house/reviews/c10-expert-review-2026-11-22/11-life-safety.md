# 11 · Life safety review of C10

Concept stage. Code-like numbers are `assumption`; a licensed professional must check them.

## Summary

1. Escape is good: every floor has a door at floor level to open air (road: front door; main: entry
   door and terrace ring; lower: lounge and grandparents' slider).
2. Falls are the main danger: about 15 open edges drop 1.0–7.8 m, and almost none has a guard drawn.
3. The open three-storey stair carries garage smoke and CO to every floor through one door.
4. The sunk arrival court and the shared south footpath leave the house exposed where it is hidden.
5. Two outdoor stairs are unsafe as drawn in a snowy climate.

## Finding details

**SAF-01 (BLOCKER).** The forecourt (1677.40) ends at v 11.6. For u −1.2…0 the arrival court
(1673.60) lies right below it: a **3.80 m** drop. The door spans v 10.15–11.35, so the drop starts
0.25 m from the jamb. For u −6.3…−2.4 the edge drops 1.0–2.6 m to natural ground
(zg(−4, 11.6) = 1676.30). No guard is drawn. All measured. *Fix:* a 1.10 m solid parapet or vertical-bar
guard (assumption) along v 11.6 from u −6.3 to 0, leaving out only the stair head. Guard the full top of any wider dug court's wall.

**SAF-02 (BLOCKER).** The green roof and the roof terrace share an open edge at v 6.2
(u 12.2–17.8), both at 1677.45. The green roof's north edge (v 1.1) drops 4.76, 6.14 and 7.80 m at
u 9.4, 13.2 and 17.8; its east edge drops 3.87 m to the master balcony. All measured. *Fix:* a 1.10 m non-climbable guard along v 6.2 and
u 12.2 (v 6.2–7.3) with a locked 0.8 m maintenance gate. Add a 1.10 m parapet on the green roof's
v 1.1 and u 17.8 edges, or at least harness anchors.

**SAF-03 (BLOCKER).** `gs-court` drops 1673.60 → 1671.30 over 2.00 m: 49° (measured). *Fix:* 14 risers × 0.164 m with goings of 0.30 m, plus a 1.2 m landing = 5.1 m of
run. Continue as one stepped route down to the lawn: 6.0 m in 37 risers of 0.162 m, with a landing at
least every 12 risers (ai_judgment). Handrails on both sides; guards wherever a side drops more than
0.6 m.

**SAF-04 (MAJOR).** One flight of 22 risers (0.173 m) and 21 goings (0.276 m) with no landing: a slip
on ice at the top is a 3.8 m fall. Sides drop 2.88 m to the court on the east at v 13 and 1.39 m on
the west (zg(−2.4, 13) = 1675.09). All measured. *Fix:* risers ≤ 0.165 m and goings ≥ 0.30 m outdoors,
at most 12 risers per flight (ai_judgment). That gives 24 × 0.158 m in 2 flights with a 1.2 m landing,
7.8 m of run (22 × 0.30 + 1.2). That does not fit 5.8 m, so move the stair west along the road, as the owner suggests. 1.10 m guards where a side drops more than 0.6 m; handrails on both sides at 0.90 m.

**SAF-05 (MAJOR).** The garage door opens into `lobby-r`, which is open 2.6 m onto the stair. The stair
is open 2.6 m on the main floor and 1.2 m on the lower floor. The bike bay (5.4–6.75, 7.0–7.8) is beside
the door. The workbench backs the 6.2 m wall shared with the stair. In cold weather, cars warmed up behind closed shutters are a classic CO case
(ai_judgment). *Fix:* keep one door (rule 5.1), but make it self-closing and fire-resisting with smoke
seals (≈ 30 min, assumption). Drop the garage floor 20 mm at the door, to a channel drain that falls
toward the shutters: this keeps the route step-free (rule 5.4). Make the core walls and the slab
fire-resisting (≈ 60 min, assumption), fuel-tight and free of service holes. Add low and high vents in
the west wall and a timed extract fan. Fit a garage CO alarm interlinked with the house. Move e-bike
charging to the shutter end (u 0.4–1.5).

**SAF-06 (MAJOR).** The fuel is unknown. If it is a gas boiler (inferred, common in the region), its
1.2 m door opens onto the road-level hall, 4 m from the front door. *Fix:* a room-sealed boiler flued
through the south wall (v 13.3), with air from outside only. A self-closing fire door (≈ 30 min,
assumption). CO alarms. The gas meter and shut-off valve outside, at the
forecourt.

**SAF-07 (MAJOR).** At every level the 2.6 m opening (u 6.8–9.4) gives onto a void over a flight:
3.9 m deep at road level and 3.6 m on the main floor. The well between the flights drops 7.5 m from
road to lower. *Fix:* a 1.10 m guard on every void edge. Fill the well with a solid spine wall, or keep
it ≤ 0.10 m. Vertical balusters ≤ 0.10 m, no horizontal infill. Handrails on both sides
of each 1.15 m flight.

**SAF-08 (MAJOR).** All drops measured:
- South terrace edge (v 16.4): 3.46 m at u 9.9, 5.76 m at u 17.
- Balcony edges (u 19.4): 3.6 m to the garden terrace, 6.0 m over the 14 steps, 4.2–4.5 m at v 1.1–2.8.
- Roof-terrace edges: 3.87 m.

The loungers stand 0.5 m from the south guard, and the chairs of the balcony table (18.5, 7.9) about
0.2 m from the edge. *Fix:* 1.10 m guards of laminated glass or vertical bars on every edge. Keep a
0.6 m strip clear of furniture at each guard: move the loungers to v ≤ 15.3, and replace the balcony
table with a fold-down shelf on the u 17.8 wall. Fit 0.10 m restrictors on the master's north windows
(3.7 m above the ground).

**SAF-09 (MAJOR).** All drops measured:
- Garden terrace (1669.98): east edge u 20.6, 2.38 m to the lawn; north edge v 2.8, 1.0–1.4 m; edge
  v 11.6, 2.38 m.
- Loggia: south edge 2.16 m (zg(17, 16.4) = 1667.82); east edge up to 2.38 m into the steps.
- The 14 steps: open on both sides.
- Play lawn: downhill edge 1.07–1.35 m.

*Fix:* 1.10 m guards on all of these. A 1.2 m ball-stop mesh fence on the lawn's downhill edge.
Handrails on the 14 steps, with goings of 0.30 m (start at v 12.5).

**SAF-10 (MAJOR).** The children's door route is good: 3 m to the lounge's outside doors, without
passing the stair. The second way out is the south window. Outside ground lies 0.69 m below the boys'
sill (zg(9.9, 16.4) = 1670.21) and 1.83 m below the girls' sill (measured). *Fix:* in each room, one
openable casement with a clear opening ≥ 0.45 × 0.75 m, ≥ 0.33 m² (assumption), and sill ≤ 0.90 m.
Put a 1.2 m level landing of `path-s` outside each. Any grille must be hinged, with a keyless release
on the inside.

**SAF-11 (MAJOR).** `court-w` lies 3.8 m below the road and cannot be seen from it, and the stair from
the road has no gate. The court continues at the same level into the south terrace and the balconies.
That one deck reaches the entry door, 5 sliders (10.8 m of glass, including the master's) and
7 windows. The lower garden adds 3 sliders and 4 windows. *Fix:*
- a 1.8 m lockable gate with an intercom at the head of the stair from the road;
- a 1.8 m wall or railing on the court's south edge v 17.4, which drops 1.27 m there (zg(3, 17.4) = 1672.33);
- motion lights and a camera on the court;
- laminated glass and multipoint locks on every slider.

Owner decision: a gate at u 4.6 between the court and the south terrace (security against rule 2.4).

**SAF-12 (MAJOR).** The south boundary is 2.0 m from the children's windows. Footpath ground is
≈ 1669.9 at the boys' window, so a passer-by's eye sits 0.6 m above the sill (measured). The north
strip (v 0–1.1) is an open walk from the road down to the garden. *Fix:* a 1.8–2.0 m wall or fence on
v 18.42 (first confirm who owns the footpath and any rights of way). Gates on the north strip at u ≈ 0
and at the lower garden. Obscure glazing on the lower half of the boys' window.

**SAF-13 (MAJOR).** The stair works without power (rule 5.2). The grandparents' only step-free way off
the lower floor is the lift. *Fix:*
- an automatic rescue device that lowers the car to the nearest stop and opens the doors;
- an alarm with a GSM link;
- a battery for about 10 trips;
- a written manual-release routine;
- manual cranks on the garage shutters.

The garden terrace is the grandparents' step-free refuge in a fire.

**SAF-14 (MINOR).** The kitchen opening is on the master's route to the stair, but the master's
balcony ring gives a second way out. *Fix:* alarms interlinked across all floors: smoke in the halls and
at the stair heads, heat in the kitchen, CO in the garage, the plant room and both bedroom halls. A fire
blanket, one extinguisher per floor, and a flame-failure gas hob or induction. Add an outdoor tap on each level.

**SAF-15 (MINOR).** `path-s` faces south, so it thaws by day and refreezes at night (inferred), and the
terrace drip line at v 16.4 falls onto it. *Fix:* gutters on every edge above a route, textured and
drained treads, step lighting, and a cover over the head of the stair from the road.

**SAF-16 (MINOR).** The grandparents' north window opens into a dark slot 1.7 m deep under the master
suite (u 14.2–18, v 1.1–2.8). *Fix:* a lockable grille at u 18, or a fixed laminated window.

## What C10 gets right

- Every floor exits to open air at floor level, so no escape depends on the stair.
- The master suite has a second way out over the balcony ring.
- The children's hall is square and 3 m from two outside doors.
- The grandparents have a step-free slider onto a refuge terrace.
- The garage-to-lift route is indoors (rule 5.1), and the stair works without power (rule 5.2).
- The 14 garden steps sit under the east balcony, out of the snow.

- Access: both outdoor stairs need re-planning.
