## Finding details

**REG-01: jurisdiction.**
- Evidence (`inferred`): `reviews/location-research/epsg-32638-preview/osm-place-context.json`.
- For land outside city limits and village boundaries, the 1391 regulation leaves coverage and density to the provincial council. In villages without a Hadi plan, Bonyad Maskan writes interim rules ([ekhtebar.ir](https://www.ekhtebar.ir/آیین-نامه-استفاده-از-اراضی،-احداث-بنا-2/)).
- `assumption`: rural regimes often allow only 2 storeys. Agricultural land first needs a land-use change permit.
- Correction: the owner obtains the lot's official planning answer (*estelam*) before C11 freezes.

**REG-02: storeys and height** (`measured`; roof slab top 1680.80).

|---|---|
| West | 3.65–6.4 m, 1 storey above the forecourt |
| North | 8.1 m (u 9.4) / 8.2 m (u 17.8) |

| South | 9.75 m at the road block (9, 13.3) |
| Overall | 1680.80 − 1667.48 = **13.3 m** |

- All three floors stack over 58.7 m².
- The lower ceiling (1673.90) is 3.1 m above the mean natural ground under it (1670.82). Under a typical basement test (ceiling ≤ 1.0–1.5 m above ground, `assumption`), it counts as a storey.
- Correction:
  - Ask how storeys and height are measured on a slope: from the street datum, from mean ground, or per façade.
  - Add a "storeys per façade" table to C11.
  - If the cap is 2 exposed storeys, the road block east of u 5 is at risk; that is an owner decision.
