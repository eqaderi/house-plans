# C10 review 13: privacy, acoustics, family and cultural life (PRV)

Concept stage. Recorded: privacy "normal", prayer room "not decided"; rule 6.7 asks for a screened wudu and a prayer bay. Cultural readings beyond these are `ai_judgment`.

## Summary

1. Guests, family and the public share one hall, one terrace and one wet cluster, with no threshold.
2. The south terrace runs level and unscreened into the public arrival court.
3. Children's beds lie under south windows; a walker's eyes are 0.5–1.2 m above the boys' sill.
4. Noise sits over or beside sleeping rooms: terrace over the children, two TVs on bedroom walls, games among bedroom doors.
5. Every fix is local; the massing does not change.
