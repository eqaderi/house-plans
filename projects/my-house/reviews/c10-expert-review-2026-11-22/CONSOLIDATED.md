## 1. Verdict in plain words

C10 has the right plan idea, but parts of it cannot be built as drawn.

- **"Garage stays on top of what?"** In C10, only half of it is held up. 28.9 of its 57.1 m² sits on the main floor's pantry, WC/wudu cluster and hall. The other 28.2 m² (the 2 m strip along the road and the north-west corner) sits on nothing designed: 0.4–4.1 m of air or loose fill over the slope. Its north and west walls have no wall under them (measured, 01/02/03/15/16). **C11:** the garage sits wholly on the main floor. A buried service room (water tanks, plant, bulk store, about 24 m²) is dug under the rest of it, and RC walls on u 0 and v 1.1 go down to natural ground.
- **"Main floor stays on top of what?"** In C10, 119 of 177 m² stands on lower-floor rooms and about 31 m² on dug ground. The other ~27 m² stands on nothing: the master suite's north strip (18.7 m², 0.9–3.9 m above the slope) and the living room's south-east corner over the open loggia, on posts 5–6 m tall. The whole south façade (v 13.0) also lands mid-span on the children's ceilings. **C11:** the lower floor's north wall moves out to v 1.1. The main and lower east walls share one line at u 18.0. Continuous columns stand on v 13.0 and at the loggia corner. Every main-floor wall then stands on a wall or column that runs to a footing.
- **Also physically wrong:**
  - The court-to-garden "stair" drops 2.30 m in a 2.0 m run.
  - The road stair is 22 risers in one flight with no landing, beside a 3.8 m drop.
  - The forecourt edge, 0.25 m from the front door, has no guard.
  - Terraces are drawn flush with the floors, so there is no depth for waterproofing and insulation.
  - Road water runs into the garage, and the sunk court has no outlet.
  - Sewage has no route out.
  - The tanks sit above the kitchen, and the hob has no exhaust duct.
- **Right, and kept:** the central stacked stair and lift core; day rooms and children's rooms facing SSE; services in the buried north-west; modest digging (deepest cut 3.97 m); the owner's 0109-r5 rooms; and a house that fits the field.
- **C11 must:**
  1. Answer "what is under it" for every slab, and draw it.
  2. Build the owner's full arrival-court dig, with a 24-riser L-stair against the road (court 34.0 → 51.6 m²).
  3. Replace the court-to-garden link with a 150 mm-riser stair that reaches the lower floor and the garden.
  4. Design the water, the terrace build-ups and the services.
  5. Fix the rooms: dining for 8, ensuite straight off the bedroom, a separate household store, a clear lift landing.

## 7. Checks C11 must add to its rule checker

Each check fails the design, and so blocks presentation under rule 8.1. Every check reads `design.py`, the same model the drawings use.

1. **Support (rule 4.9).** Every slab region (each plate, terrace, balcony, the garage and the forecourt deck) has a wall, a column line or natural ground within 0.3 m beneath it. Report the m² on each kind of support per floor. Fail on any unsupported area >0.5 m², and on any cantilever >1.4 m beyond its support line.
2. **Wall over wall.** Upper load-bearing walls lie within ±0.10 m of a wall or beam line below. List every offset of 0.10–0.50 m, and every wall landing mid-span.
3. **One set of supports.** Plans, sections and 3D draw the same columns, foundation walls and retaining walls, and `supports()` invents none. Every ground step >0.3 m has a drawn wall with top and bottom levels. Sections cut every outdoor stair.
4. **Fill under slabs.** Fill depth under any ground-bearing slab ≤1.0 m (otherwise it must be marked suspended). Earthworks count the voids under slabs. Report the deepest cut to formation against ~4.5 m.
5. **Outdoor stairs (rule 4.11).**<br>- riser ≤0.175 m (target 0.150–0.160);<br>- going ≥0.28 m (target ≥0.30);<br>- 2R+G 0.60–0.66 m;<br>- ≤16 risers per flight (target ≤12);<br>- landings ≥1.2 m, at the top and the bottom;<br>- width ≥1.0 m (target 1.2 m);<br>- run computed from `z`/`z_end`: fail if the drop needs more run than drawn;<br>- handrails both sides.
6. **Internal stair.** One going on every flight, and the riser count matches the drawing.
7. **Guards.** Every edge with a drop ≥0.6 m has a guard ≥1.1 m, and a 0.6 m furniture-free strip in front of it.
8. **Terrace set-down.** Every terrace or roof over a heated room has a structural set-down ≥ (terrace build-up − floor build-up), and a drain at each door. A flush slab fails.
9. **Water.** Every outdoor surface has a fall direction, ≥2 outlets plus an overflow, and no fall towards a door. The forecourt and garage crests sit above the surveyed road edge. The court has an outlet.
10. **Clear routes.**<br>- Door clear widths, and a 1.2 m route checked against furniture and parked cars (garage aisle ≥1.2 m at u 5.54–6.74).<br>- A 1.5 m circle clear in front of every lift door.<br>- No door swing collides with furniture.
11. **Bedroom windows (rule 4.8).** At least 2 m in front of each bedroom window, at or below the sill. Sample 0.3–2.0 m out, on finished ground, including paths, stairs and platforms.
12. **Windows into voids.** No window faces an enclosed void or plinth, or a wall within 2.0 m. The daylight check skips such windows.
13. **Setback with thickness.** Every element, including its wall thickness, stays inside the envelope.
14. **Real walls.** `room_dims()` uses 0.40 m / 0.45 m exterior walls. Living ≥5.0 m clear (rule 3.3). Kitchen 15–17 m², pantry 4–5 m², laundry 4–6 m² with a separate store room. Dining fits a 2.2 × 1.0 m table with 0.9 m all round.
15. **Services.** Every wet room is ≤4.5 m from a riser. Every windowless room has an extract route to a riser. The hob sits under a duct that rises through non-habitable space to the roof. No WC or tank stands over the kitchen.
16. **Safety doors present (rule 2.4).** The garage fire door, and the road-level glazed stair screen with its door. Any other new door is flagged for justification.
17. **WC sightlines.** A ray test from the hall and the kitchen openings to every WC pan gives 0 hits.
18. **Qibla and north.** A qibla arrow and true north on every plan. Mats within ±5° of the qibla.
19. **Lift.** Clear shaft ≥1.80 × 2.60 m; pit and overrun drawn.
20. **Text matches geometry.** Every area and distance quoted in notes or the README is recomputed. `checks.json` may mark a 0110-r2 defect fixed only through a measured test.
21. **Owner levels.** Road FFL 1677.50, main 1673.60, lower 1670.00: any change is flagged as an owner decision.
22. **Storeys per façade** (REG-02) and coverage (plates, terraces, filled courts) are reported.
