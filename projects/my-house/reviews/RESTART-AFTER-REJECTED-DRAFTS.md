## Useful lessons retained, without rejected design geometry

- **ai_judgment / method:** Test the house and usable garden together against the real skew boundary and terrain. A terrain sampling window alone does not demonstrate a simultaneous architectural fit. Large raised outdoor planes can create substantial retaining/support implications; assess these anew for each future design.
- **ai_judgment / method:** Do not let default room bars, repeated galleries or solver convenience determine the architecture. The deleted studies exceeded the initial advisory area bands and were not accepted. Numerical areas/storeys/finished levels from them are not owner preferences or future constraints.
- **measured software limitation:** The current generic step-free evaluator traverses passable openings but does not model vertical lift travel. A separate assumed-lift route graph is only a supplementary check, not a reason to erase hard findings or claim accessibility approval.
- **measured software limitation:** The 2D assembler's ordinary floor/roof slab construction uses room bounding rectangles; L-shaped space exports need exact polygon derivation and checks, including stair openings. A passing room-geometry check does not independently prove faithful slab geometry.
- **ai_judgment / review:** For shared children's rooms test two full beds, not a single default fixture. Furniture-body fit alone does not establish comfortable circulation, wheelchair maneuvering or usable room design.

These are process/implementation observations, not a surviving design recommendation. Survey-derived measurements, assumptions and unresolved professional matters retain their existing epistemic status. No need to repeat location discovery or alter survey evidence merely because the plans were rejected.
