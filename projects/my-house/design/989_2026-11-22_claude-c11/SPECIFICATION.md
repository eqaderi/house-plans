# C11 outline specification

What the drawings cannot show: build-ups, materials, services and safety items that `CORRECTIONS.md` marks "Partly · specified here". Each line names its review row. Figures are concept targets and `assumption` unless a source is given; an engineer, a services designer and the local authority set the final values. Nothing here is engineered, permit-ready or code-compliant.

## Structure and ground

- **Loads for the engineer (STR-08, STR-10):** snow 1.5–2.5 kPa plus drift 1.9–2.6 kPa at the 3.4 m roof step (assumption, INBR Part 6 to confirm); garage floor ≥2.5 kPa plus a point load; forecourt and its deck for vehicles up to 16 t (assumption: deliveries, construction laydown); green roof extensive, substrate ≤150 mm (≤2.5 kPa saturated); deck pavers on pedestals.
- **Lateral system (STR-06, SEI-03):** RC dual system; RC lift shaft and stair core with a 0.15 m RC spine between the flights; collector beams on v 1.1 and v 7.3; one end of each flight on a sliding bearing. The engineer checks the stiffness-centre / mass-centre offset (target ≤0.1 × plan dimension) and decides whether the uphill retaining walls stay integral or become free-standing (see the open decisions).
- **Footings (STR-07):** stepped (steps ≤0.5 m) to undisturbed ground below frost depth (unknown; assume ≥0.8 m), tie-beam grid; piers or a movement joint near u 13 if the north-west is on rock and the south-east on soil.
- **Earthworks sequence (GEO-05):** condition surveys of both neighbours and utility locates → strip and keep ~22 m³ of topsoil → north pile wall PW-N and road-side shoring before any dig → dig top-down west → east in lifts ≤1.5 m (batter only east and south), June–September → lift pit, basement walls, tanking and drains before the wet season → frame bottom-up; backfill only after the slabs are cast → garden walls last. Laydown on the forecourt; long-reach excavator from the road for the upper dig; skips craned from the road for the lower pit. About 400–500 m³ in place to remove (397 m³ modelled plus footings, piles, stairs and sub-bases).

## Water and waterproofing

- **Buried walls (WAT-04):** watertight RC (waterstops), external membrane, drainage board, XPS 100 mm over the top 1.5 m and 60 mm below; DN110 footing drain (invert ~1669.35, 1:100 to a catch pit at (18.6, 13.0)); 150 mm stone blanket under the lower slab, draining east; pantry and store walls tanked, shelves 50 mm off the walls.
- **Terraces over rooms (WAT-03):** inverted roof: 2-layer SBS (root-resistant under the green roof), XPS 140–200 mm, drainage mat, pedestal pavers finishing 15–20 mm below FFL; upstands 150 mm (50 mm at sliders with a 150 mm slot channel); falls 1:60–1:80 away from the house; 48 h flood test. Balconies and the loggia roof uninsulated (~0.15 m build-up), edge gutter, drips, side-fixed rails.
- **North strip (WAT-06):** lined channel at v ~0.3 cast into the pile cap, over a solid DN110, drop catch pits at u 2, 6.8, 11.4 and 16; heat-traced outlets.
- **Discharge (WAT-07):** nothing piped across a boundary; cistern 10–15 m³ under the garden terrace (irrigation, WC flushing, fire hose); overflow to a level spreader ≥3 m from the east boundary (position after a percolation test); lower-floor greywater to subsurface drip June–September only.
- **Wet rooms over rooms (WAT-09):** road WC and plant room on tanked floors with a 100–150 mm upstand and a gully; level showers in a 0.20–0.25 m slab recess; no pipe joint in a kitchen ceiling.
- **Frost (WAT-10):** downpipes inside the warm envelope with overflow scuppers; pipes ≥0.8 m deep or insulated; no uninsulated pipe in the garage.

## Arrival, stairs, garage

- **Road walls (GEO-03, fix round):** W2, W3 and the road-stair flights as one drained RC U-trough, walls 0.35 m on a common base at about 1673.0 with a shear key, the flights strutting between them, counterforts ≤3.0 m apart; designed for traffic (≈10 kPa, assumption) plus seismic load. W2 monolithic up to about 1678.30 as the vehicle parapet. Road-stair head: 30 mm hump at the gate, 150 mm slot drain across the head, treads with a 1–2 % forward fall.
- **North pile wall (GEO-01, fix round):** secant Ø450 piles on v 0.40, toes 1666.5 (u 0–4), 1664.5 (u 4–11.4), 1667.0 (u 11.4–16), all to be re-sized after boreholes; capping beam 0.2 m under the ground, stepping with it; 0.2 m drained cavity to the house wall with membrane, XPS and a DN110 toe drain.
- **Outdoor stairs (ARR-07, MAT-09):** flamed local limestone treads (absorption ≤0.5 %, wet PTV ≥36, assumption) with integral nosing and a 1–2 % forward fall; drained landings; step lights; canopy over L1/F2 of the road stair; handrails both sides at 0.9–1.0 m, guards 1.1 m.
- **Garage (VEH-02, VEH-04, VEH-05):** wheel stops; RCD circuit; steel fuel cabinet; fire door ~30–60 min (assumption), smoke seals, self-closer and an alarm-released hold-open; extract 20 l/s continuous and 150 l/s for 15 min after a shutter moves, with a 0.05 m² low inlet; interlinked CO alarms; 100 mm insulation under the garage slab (the garage is the ceiling of 57 m² of rooms) and 80 mm on shared walls; insulated shutters with a soffit at 1680.50, coil box 0.35–0.45 m, battery or manual override, wind locks; door operator ≥0.2 m/s.
- **Lift (CIR-02):** machine-room-less, automatic rescue device, battery for ≥5–10 trips, GSM alarm, 1.5 t lifting hook; lobby doors close-fitting so garage air cannot reach the shaft.
- **Stair (CIR-03):** 1.1 m guards on every void edge, balusters ≤0.10 m apart, handrails both sides; obscured north window on the road → main half-landing (sill 1676.45); operable rooflight about 2.2 × 1.2 m with rain sensor; 0.9 m roof hatch; battery step lights and emergency lighting; evacuation chair at the lower foot; fixings for a future stair-lift.

## Services

- **Ventilation (MEP-04):** MVHR; extract WC 6–15 l/s, showers, wudu and ensuites 15–25 l/s with a 65 % RH boost, pantry 5–10, laundry 10 / 30; supply ≥15 l/s per two-person bedroom; transfer grilles or door undercuts; terminals on the road or green roof, never on the east façade, >3 m from the roof terrace.
- **Heat and hot water (MEP-05):** heat-loss calculation first (about 16–19 kW with a good envelope, inferred); 18–24 kW room-sealed condensing boiler or a heat pump; 300 L solar-ready cylinder; underfloor heating at 35–40 °C on the lower and main floors, radiators at road level; hot-water return loop on a timer; 10 L heater at the tea bar; gas meter, seismic shut-off, stop-cock and main isolator in an outside niche; kitchen gas detector; split heat pumps for cooling; 250 × 250 mm flue chase from the lounge for a backup stove. Boiler flue: vertical, in its own sleeve in the plant room, ≥1.0 m above the roof, never in the kitchen grease riser SH-K.
- **Power (MEP-06):** 3-phase (assumption); meters at the forecourt boundary; essential circuit on a 3–5 kVA battery inverter (foul pump, booster, boiler controls, lift rescue, lighting); PV 7–8 kWp on the road roof; 10–15 kWh battery in a fire-separated cupboard; 32 A generator inlet; foundation earth electrode; lightning ring; Type 1+2 surge protection; EV conduit.
- **Radon (MEP-07):** 150 mm gravel gas layer in the 0.60 m formation; 0.4 mm membrane lapped up the earth walls; sumps at (10.4, 9.4) and (5.9, 13.0) on a Ø110 pipe up SH-W, passive and fan-ready; 3-month winter tests in all four bedrooms; act at 100 Bq/m³ (WHO).
- **Foul outfall (MEP-01):** A road sewer (sealed duplex lifting station with ≥1.2 m³ emergency volume on the battery circuit, rising main to a break chamber under the forecourt), B a gravity drain downhill (preferred if an easement exists), or C a holding tank. Decide after the sewer survey (F3).
- **WCs (KB-04):** every WC has a shattaf with hot/cold mixer, a Ø50 floor drain in front of the pan, a tiled dado, a slip-resistant floor.

## Envelope

- **Envelope (THM-02):** walls ~0.30 W/m²K, roofs ~0.20, windows Uw ≤1.3 (≤1.0 on ENE and NNW); airtightness n50 ≤3/h; each floor zoned; insulation outside the buried walls; 120 mm under exposed floors and over the crawl voids, with a closed perimeter.
- **Thermal bridges (THM-03, THM-06):** balconies self-supporting on their own columns, connected to the house with thermal-break connectors (ψ ≤0.2).
- **Snow (THM-04):** road-roof parapets 0.5–0.6 m with snow guards; canopies 1.0–1.5 m over the east doors and the entries; nobody shovels onto walking routes.
- **Crawl voids:** vented to outside on two faces (3 mm mesh), 0.6 × 0.6 m access hatch, membrane on the ground, a drain at the low point.

## Exterior and materials

- **Materials (EXT-05, MAT):** local limestone 30–40 mm, dry-fixed on A4 stainless anchors; stone plinth ≥600 mm and a stone base for the lower floor and garden walls up to about 1673.3 (EXT-01); lime render or ETICS above; timber only for replaceable shutters and pergola slats; aluminium lift-and-slide sliders; duplex-coated steel rails; exposed concrete air-entrained C35/45, w/c ≤0.45, cover 45–50 mm, XF4 at the forecourt, garage and road stair; no travertine, soft sandstone, polished marble or glazed tile outdoors.
- **Guards (EXT-03):** 1.1 m solid parapet with a 0.45 m planter on the south terrace; 0.3 m upstand with a 0.8 m flat-bar rail on the east balconies; 1.1 m solid on the roof terrace; 0.5 m stone plus a rail on the garden terrace; RC vehicle parapet + rail on the road edge above the road stair.

## Safety and security

- **Security (SAF-03):** laminated inner pane (44.2) and multipoint locks on every slider and low pane; external roller shutters on the bedroom windows; motion lights and a camera on the court; gates on the north strip and at the lower garden.
- **After an earthquake or an outage (SAF-04):** outside shut-off niche; assembly points at the road and on the NE lawn; 72 h kit with 72 L of water in a garage locker; anchored furniture and PV; manual cranks on the shutters.
- **Wildfire (SAF-05):** 1.5 m gravel strip round the house, 10 m of irrigated low-fuel planting, deciduous broadleaf trees with crowns ≥3 m from the façades, no timber decks, 3 mm ember mesh on vents.
- **Detection (SAF-06):** interlinked smoke alarms in the halls and stair heads, heat in the kitchen, CO in the garage, the plant room and both bedroom halls; an extinguisher on each floor and a fire blanket; fire-resisting core walls and slab (~60 min, assumption).

## Flexibility

- **FLX-01:** lintels for future glazed doors at the main stair opening (v 7.3, u 6.8–9.4) and the lower one (u 8.2–9.4); key switches on the lift calls; a 32 A circuit and a duct route at the tea bar.
