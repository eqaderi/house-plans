Weak points I know about

  • The parents/grandparents room (lower, south-west) has one 1.4 m window, and the ground outside is just below the sill.
  • The garage apron needs about 1.4 m of build-up, carried by the forecourt slab. The road edge itself is level with the garage floor.
  • Upstairs, the lift opens into the small stair hall, which is open to the living room.
  • Total size is about 398 m² indoors, partly because you chose the bigger upper floor.
  • Room sizes are my proposal. They are not yet an approved program.

Questions for you

1. Weak room. Parents/grandparents room: swap with the girls' room (south-east corner), swap with the family room, or keep it as is?
2. Size. About 398 m² indoors. Keep it, or trim it (for example the garage-store strip or a smaller family room)?
3. Lift door upstairs. Is it okay that it opens into the stair hall (open to the living room), not straight into the living room?
4. Arriving on foot. Road → stair down along the road border → sunken court → covered porch → entrance hall. Is that okay?
