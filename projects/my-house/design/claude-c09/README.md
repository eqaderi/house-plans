# Claude C09: compact versions of C08 that fit the field

C08 was too big (stmt-023). The owner chose, in stmt-024, a ~150 m² east garden (lower floor ends at u ≈ 18), a living room of ~34 m² with the dining opening into it, and digging of at most ~4.5 m. These are exploratory sketches; nothing is stored in `ProjectStore`. Open `index.html`.

| Option | Master suite | Lower floor |
|---|---|---|
| C09-1 | mid-north, private NE terrace | grandparents NE, garden lounge east |
| C09-2 | NE corner, walk-in dress → ensuite | garden lounge NE, grandparents east |

Rebuild: `../../../../.venv/bin/python build.py`. The engine, checks and report were copied from `../claude-c08`; the geometry is in `options.py`.
